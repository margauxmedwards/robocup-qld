✨ New to RoboCup?

RoboCup Junior is the world's largest robotics program for school students. Pick a challenge, build a robot, and take it to an event near you.

⚽ Soccer Autonomous robots chase an infrared ball in fast 2-on-2 matches.
🛟 Rescue Line Follow the line, dodge obstacles and rescue victims from the danger zone.
🧭 Rescue Maze Map an unknown maze and find victims — no line to guide you.
🎭 OnStage Robot theatre! Choreograph a performance with robots as the stars.
🥋 Sumo The beginner-friendly entry league — push your opponent out of the ring.
🧱 SPIKE Prime Challenge A virtual challenge built around the LEGO SPIKE Prime platform.
Who can join? School-aged students from primary to Year 12, in school or community teams. Any platform is welcome — LEGO, micro:bit, Arduino or fully custom builds — and regional events are a friendly place to start before State and Nationals.
