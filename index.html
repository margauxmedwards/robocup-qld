About the program

What is RoboCup?

Hands-on robotics for every student

RoboCup Junior is an international robotics education program where students design, build and program fully autonomous robots to solve real-world challenges. Students compete in local, state and national events, with no qualification required — every event is open to all teams.

The program combines hands-on learning with exciting robotics competitions, helping students explore STEM in a fun and engaging way. Any platform is welcome — LEGO, Arduino, micro:bit or fully custom builds.

Why schools join

  • Robotics and coding skills
  • Engineering and design thinking
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Collaboration and teamwork
  • Confidence in STEM learning

The competition supports curriculum-aligned project learning across Science, Digital Technologies, Engineering, and the Arts. Workshops and resources are available to help schools get started.

The challenges

Pick your arena

Four consistent challenges, from first-time teams through to advanced robotics.

Start here · Queensland entry challenge Sumo

Build an autonomous robot that competes in robot sumo matches by detecting and pushing opponents out of the arena. A beginner-friendly challenge designed to help students get started with robotics before progressing to the RoboCup Junior leagues.

Soccer Design autonomous robots that compete in fast-paced robot soccer matches.
Rescue Line Program a robot to follow a complex path and deliver aid to victims in a simulated disaster environment.
Rescue Maze Build a fully autonomous robot capable of navigating a maze and locating victims.
OnStage Combine robotics, creativity and storytelling in a robot-assisted performance.
